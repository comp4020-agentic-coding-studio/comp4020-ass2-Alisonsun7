# Lectures

Two lectures cover the SEE stage of the course. [Lecture
1](/lectures/week-01/) opens the semester with the five readings of "price"
and carries a full slide deck; [Lecture 2](/lectures/week-02/) spans Weeks 2
and 3 together — unit price and reference pricing are one argument in two
parts, and the lecture treats them as such. Weeks 4 onward run without a
matching lecture: the CALCULATE and DECIDE stages are taught through the
weekly sessions and their interactive tools instead.
