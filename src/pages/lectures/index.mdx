# Lectures

Four lectures span the course's three stages. [Lecture 1](/lectures/week-01/)
opens the SEE stage with the five readings of "price"; [Lecture 2](/lectures/week-02/)
spans Weeks 2 and 3 together, since unit price and reference pricing are one
argument in two parts. [Lecture 3](/lectures/week-04/) opens the CALCULATE
stage by modelling coupon rules as functions rather than tricks, and
[Lecture 4](/lectures/week-10/) opens the DECIDE stage with the expected-value
case for (and against) waiting. Every lecture carries a full slide deck; the
remaining weeks are taught through their sessions and interactive tools
directly.
