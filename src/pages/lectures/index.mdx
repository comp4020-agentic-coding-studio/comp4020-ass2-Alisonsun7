# Lectures

Four lectures cover the big turns in the course. [Lecture 1](/lectures/week-01/)
opens the SEE stage with five meanings of "price". [Lecture 2](/lectures/week-02/)
joins Weeks 2 and 3, because unit price and discount claims are two versions
of the same question: what is this number being compared to? [Lecture
3](/lectures/week-04/) starts the CALCULATE stage by treating coupon rules as
small if-this-then-that rules. [Lecture 4](/lectures/week-10/) starts the
DECIDE stage by asking when waiting for a lower price is worth the risk. Each
lecture has a full slide deck; the other weeks are taught through their session
pages and interactive tools.
