# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

Work submitted after the due date attracts standard SlopU late penalties
unless an extension has been agreed in advance. Ask early — a five-minute
email before a deadline is a much easier conversation than one after it.

## Academic integrity

Submitted work must be your own reasoning, with any tools or assistance used
declared. [Assessment 1](/assessments/deal-autopsy/) in particular asks you
to investigate a real promotion: cite where you found it, and don't present
a classmate's chosen promotion or analysis as your own.

## Hypothetical vs sourced examples

This course traces one recurring example — a pair of $249 headphones —
through the whole semester. Some weeks illustrate a concept with **sourced**
figures (real, citable prices or public information) and some with
**hypothetical** ones invented purely to demonstrate a calculation. Content
in this course labels which is which; treat any unlabelled invented figure
as a bug worth reporting, not as a real-world claim. [Week
5](/sessions/week-05/) and [Week 6](/sessions/week-06/) are the two weeks
that lean on this distinction most heavily — both need invented data to teach
a concept this course has no real dataset for.

## Getting help

Use the weekly session for anything about that week's material or worked
examples. Use email for anything course-level, or that can't wait until the
next session — see [People](/people/) for who to contact.
