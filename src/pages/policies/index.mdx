# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

Work submitted after the due date attracts standard SlopU late penalties
unless an extension has been agreed in advance. Ask early — a five-minute
email before a deadline is a much easier conversation than one after it.

## Academic integrity

Submitted work must show your own reasoning. Declare any tools or help you
used. [Assessment 1](/assessments/deal-autopsy/) asks you to investigate a
real promotion, so cite where you found it. Do not use a classmate's chosen
promotion or analysis as your own.

## Hypothetical vs sourced examples

This course follows one recurring example: a pair of $249 headphones. Some
weeks use **sourced** figures: real prices or public information you could
cite. Other weeks use **hypothetical** figures: invented numbers used only to
show a calculation. The site labels which is which. If an invented number is
not labelled, treat that as a mistake to report, not as a real-world claim.
[Week 5](/sessions/week-05/) and [Week 6](/sessions/week-06/) rely on this
most, because they need made-up data to teach the idea safely.

## Getting help

Use the weekly session for anything about that week's material or worked
examples. Use email for anything course-level, or that can't wait until the
next session — see [People](/people/) for who to contact.
